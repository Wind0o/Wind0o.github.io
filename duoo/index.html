DuoO

一屏，多种视界。
Photo slideshows, independently in every panel.

适用于 iPhone、iPad、Mac 与 Apple TV。全局随机选图，各栏独立换片、暂停和选择相簿。多栏等比铺满，横屏单图默认完整显示，支持轻柔交叉淡入。

For iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV. Shuffle across eligible photo albums, independently control every panel, and enjoy gentle crossfades. Multiple panels fill their frames; a single photo in landscape fits by default.

支持 / Support

联系：01057@163.com

首次使用请允许照片访问。普通相簿需包含 10–1000 张图片，图片占照片与视频至少 90%。多栏使用竖图和正方形图片，单图可播放横图。默认仅播放本地图片；需要 iCloud 图片时，在设置中关闭“只播放本地图片”。Apple TV 需先在系统照片中具备可访问的相簿。

Use a regular album with 10–1000 images and at least 90% images among its media. Multiple panels use portrait and square photos. Disable local-only playback in Settings if you want Apple Photos to fetch iCloud images. Apple TV requires albums exposed by its system Photos library.

触控：轻点显示控件，上下滑动换片，长按暂停，双击收藏。Mac：空格暂停，上下换片，左右选栏，右键打开菜单。TV：左右选栏，上下换图，确认或播放键暂停。

隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

更新日期 / Updated: 2026-09-21

DuoO 无需账号，不收集个人数据，不使用广告、分析或追踪 SDK，也不将照片、相簿名称、图片标识或使用记录上传给开发者。

应用通过 Apple PhotoKit 访问你允许的系统图库，用于显示图片、筛选相簿，以及在 iPhone、iPad、Mac 上进行收藏或经系统确认的删除。你可以随时在系统设置中修改照片权限。

偏好设置、相簿索引、显示缓存和已看记录存储在设备上的独立应用容器中。可在应用设置内清除已播记录。卸载应用会移除应用容器数据，但不删除系统照片图库。

默认仅播放已下载的图片。若主动关闭本地播放限制，Apple Photos 可能通过 Apple 的服务获取 iCloud 图片；该过程由 Apple 管理，开发者不会接收你的图片或 Apple 账户信息。

DuoO requires no account, collects no personal data, and includes no advertising, analytics or tracking SDK. Photos, album names, asset identifiers and usage history are not uploaded to the developer.

Apple PhotoKit is used to access the library you authorize, display and filter photos, and favorite or delete photos on supported platforms. Deletion requires system confirmation. You can change Photos permissions in system settings.

Preferences, catalog indexes, image caches and watched history stay in the app container on your device. Watched history can be cleared in Settings. Removing the app removes its container data without deleting your system photo library. If you turn off local-only playback, Apple Photos may fetch iCloud content through Apple services; the developer does not receive those photos or your Apple account information.
