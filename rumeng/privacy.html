入梦 - 时光放映机

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 9 月 16 日

概览

入梦是一款本机优先的相簿视频播放应用。它读取你通过 Apple 照片权限授权且可访问的视频，用于生成沉浸式播放流。1.1.1 起同时通过系统“视频”智能相簿发现未加入普通相簿的视频；不读取隐藏相簿。较早版本仅从可访问的普通相簿读取视频。

入梦不要求注册账号，不接入广告 SDK 或分析 SDK，也不会把你的照片、视频或相簿内容上传到开发者服务器。

我们不收集的数据

入梦不从 App 中收集个人数据，不运营用于接收相册内容的服务器，也不出售或用于跨 App 跟踪你的信息。

照片访问与系统操作

入梦使用 Apple PhotoKit 读取你授权范围内且可访问的视频，包括普通相簿和系统“视频”智能相簿中的视频；普通相簿可能包括共享或同步相簿。双击收藏时，App 会请求修改当前视频的系统“个人收藏”状态；点击删除时，App 会请求把当前视频移入系统“最近删除”，且 iOS 会再次显示确认。你可以随时在系统设置中限制或撤销照片权限。

有限照片访问模式下，入梦只能处理你明确授权的视频。1.1.1 起可通过系统“视频”智能相簿发现已授权但未加入普通相簿的视频；入梦不读取隐藏相簿。

设备本地存储

为了维持使用体验，入梦会在 App 容器内保存播放设置、相簿索引与 PhotoKit 本机标识、每日免费片单和查看进度、已播记录，以及用于故障排查的本机播放诊断信息。这些数据不会发送到开发者服务器。删除 App 通常会同时删除其 App 容器内的数据；系统照片图库中的原视频不因此被删除。

iCloud 照片

如果视频只存放在 iCloud 照片中，Apple Photos 框架可能在播放时按需从 iCloud 获取原片。该传输由 Apple 的系统服务处理。你可以在入梦中开启“只播放本地视频”，避免 App 主动请求仅存于 iCloud 的原片。

购买与订阅

入梦 Pro 的月付、年付和永久买断均通过 Apple App Store 与 StoreKit 处理。付款信息由 Apple 处理，入梦只读取经 StoreKit 验证的购买权益，用于解锁功能。自动续期订阅可在 Apple ID 的订阅管理中取消。1.1.1 的独立 30 天免费体验也是通过 StoreKit 领取的零元项目；App 使用已验证交易的首次购买时间计算到期日，恢复购买不会重置试用，不自动扣费。

入梦采用 Apple 标准最终用户许可协议（EULA）。

儿童与政策更新

入梦不会故意收集儿童个人信息。若本政策因功能或法律要求更新，我们会在本页面修改内容和“最后更新”日期。

RuMeng Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Overview

RuMeng is a local-first album video player. It reads authorized, accessible videos through Apple Photos and presents them as an immersive feed. From version 1.1.1, the Videos smart album also helps discover videos not added to ordinary albums. Hidden albums are not read. Earlier versions read accessible ordinary albums only.

RuMeng does not require an account, include advertising or analytics SDKs, or upload your photos, videos, or album contents to developer-operated servers.

Data We Do Not Collect

RuMeng does not collect personal data from the app, operate a server that receives your photo-library content, sell your information, or use it for cross-app tracking.

Photos Access and System Actions

RuMeng uses Apple PhotoKit to read authorized, accessible videos through ordinary albums and the Videos smart album. Ordinary albums may include shared or synced albums. When you double-tap, the app requests a change to the current video's system Favorite status. When you choose delete, the app requests that the current video be moved to Recently Deleted, and iOS presents a confirmation. You may limit or revoke Photos access in Settings at any time.

With limited Photos access, RuMeng can process only videos you explicitly authorize. From version 1.1.1, the Videos smart album also allows discovery of authorized videos outside ordinary albums. RuMeng does not read Hidden albums.

On-Device Storage

RuMeng stores playback preferences, an album index and local PhotoKit identifiers, the daily free selection and progress, watched history, and local playback diagnostics inside the app container. This information is not sent to developer-operated servers. Deleting the app normally removes its container data; it does not by itself delete videos from the system photo library.

iCloud Photos

If a video is stored only in iCloud Photos, Apple's Photos framework may retrieve it on demand for playback. Apple system services handle that transfer. You may enable “Play Local Videos Only” to prevent RuMeng from intentionally requesting originals that are available only in iCloud.

Purchases and Subscriptions

Monthly, annual, and lifetime RuMeng Pro purchases are processed through the Apple App Store and StoreKit. Apple processes payment details. RuMeng reads only StoreKit-verified entitlements to unlock features. Auto-renewable subscriptions can be cancelled in Apple ID subscription settings. The separate 30-day free trial in version 1.1.1 is also claimed through StoreKit as a zero-price purchase. Its expiry uses the original verified purchase date; restoring does not restart the trial and the trial does not automatically charge you.

RuMeng uses the Apple Standard End User License Agreement (EULA).

Children and Updates

RuMeng does not knowingly collect children's personal information. If this policy changes because of product or legal requirements, this page and its last-updated date will be revised.

For privacy questions, contact 01057@163.com.
